Capybara vs. Granny

Kexiang Shuai,

Abstract

Capybara vs. Granny is a local multiplayer party game developed as a Carnegie Mellon University course project. I implemented the combat systems in Unity, including health, hit detection, death, respawn, and environmental destruction. I also built the capybara stacking mechanic, coordinating player state, physics constraints, and input timing. Over a three-week development cycle, our team completed seven prototype iterations across six rounds of playtesting.

My role: Unity Programmer.
